Methodology

This app reproduces the pipeline in new_methods.ipynb exactly, for one new audio file at a time. Nothing below is invented beyond what deployment requires.

Preprocessing

librosa.load(path, sr=22050) — mono, resampled to 22050 Hz. No trimming, padding, or amplitude normalization.

Features (concatenated into one vector)

Summary statistics (5, always computed): zero-crossing rate, RMS energy, spectral centroid, spectral roll-off (85th percentile), spectral bandwidth — each the mean over frames (hop_length=512).

Main transform (one, by feature set): MFCC (13 coefficients, n_fft=2048), STFT magnitude (1025 bins), or CQT magnitude (84 bins) — each averaged over time.

Classification

A scikit-learn Pipeline(StandardScaler → SVC(kernel="rbf", C=10, gamma="scale")), already fitted on the IRMAS training set, is loaded and only ever called with .predict(). The scaler and SVM are never refit on uploaded audio.

PCA visualization

PCA is not part of the classifier — it is used only to visualize the trained feature space in 2D. A StandardScaler and PCA(n_components=2) were fit once, offline, on the notebook's own saved training+test feature arrays; a separate 2D SVM was fit on those PCA coordinates purely to draw decision regions. New audio is projected into this space with .transform() only.
